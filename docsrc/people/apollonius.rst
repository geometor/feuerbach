Apollonius of Perga
===================

*Greek Geometer*

Author of the *Conics* and the lost *Tangencies* (*De Tactionibus*); Apollonius's median theorem in :math:`\triangle KOS` is the decisive algebraic hinge of Feuerbach's § 57 proof.
