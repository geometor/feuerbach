===============================================================================
Summary — Unit IV: Proportional Derived Triangles (§§ 61–87)
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Homothetic Hierarchies of Contact and Orthic Triangles

1. **Geometric-Mean Proportionality (§§ 61, 63)**:
   Both the orthic-contact operation and the contact-orthic operation square the scale factor :math:`\frac{\rho}{R}` or :math:`\frac{r^{(k)}}{2R}`, yielding exact area geometric means:

   .. math::

      \triangle def : \triangle MNP = \triangle MNP : \Delta, \qquad
      \mu^{(k)} : \delta^{(k)} = \delta^{(k)} : \Delta,

   while rotating side orientations back into exact **parallelism** (homothety) with :math:`\triangle ABC`!
2. **Five-Triangle Area Partition (§ 64)**:
   Combining :math:`\mu^{(k)} = \frac{r^{(k)2}}{4R^2}\Delta` with :math:`\sum r_i^2 = 4R(2R - \rho)` yields the remarkable metric conservation law

   .. math::

      \mu + \mu' + \mu'' + \mu''' \pm \triangle MNP = 2\Delta.

3. **Half-Perimeter & Corner Inradii (§§ 65, 71–73)**:
   The orthic triangle :math:`\triangle mnp` of the contact triangle :math:`\triangle DEF` has perimeter equal to **one-half** the perimeter of :math:`\triangle MNP` (:math:`\frac{\Delta}{R}`), and the corner orthic inradii :math:`\rho' = r\cos\alpha`, :math:`\rho'' = r\cos\beta`, :math:`\rho''' = r\cos\gamma` satisfy :math:`OM + ON + OP = r + \rho + \rho' + \rho'' + \rho'''`.
4. **Translation Congruence & Orthocenter S (§§ 85–87)**:
   The corner-incenter triangle :math:`\triangle O'O''O'''` is congruent and parallel-sided to :math:`\triangle DEF`, carrying the orthocenter :math:`J` of :math:`\triangle DEF` into the incenter :math:`S = X(1)` of :math:`\triangle ABC`.
