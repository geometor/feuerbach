§ 51: § 51: Squared Distance from the Orthocenter O to S (and the Excenter Sign Slip) — Summary
===============================================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *§. 51. Quadrate der Abstände OS, OS', OS'', OS'''*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 51 — Orthocenter-to-Incenter Distance**:
  Direct calculation on base :math:`c` yields the 100% exact incenter distance:

  .. math::

     \overline{OS}^2 = 2r^2 - 2\rho R.

  *(Note: Feuerbach's printed excenter formula flipped the sign of R without flipping rho; the true exact identity is OS_k^2 = 2r_k^2 - 2rho R.)*
