§§ 52–53: §§ 52–53: Sum of Orthocenter Quadrances & The Distance KO — Summary
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *§§. 52–53. Summe der Quadrate von OS und das Quadrat des Abstands KO*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§§ 52–53 — Orthocenter Sum and Exact Distance KO**:
  - **§ 52**: Sum of orthocenter squared distances to the tritangent centers: true exact sum is :math:`16R(R - \rho)`.
  - **§ 53**: Squared distance between circumcenter :math:`K` and orthocenter :math:`O`:

    .. math::

       \overline{KO}^2 = R^2 - 4\rho R = R(R - 4\rho).
