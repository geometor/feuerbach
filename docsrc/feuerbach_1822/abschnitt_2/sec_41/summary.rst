Section Overview
----------------

* **§§ 41–48 — Corner Triangles and Circumcenter Sub-Circles**:
  - Orthocenters :math:`M', N', P'` of corner triangles satisfy :math:`AM' + BN' + CP' = 2(R - \rho)`.
  - Circumcenter distances :math:`Ka = \frac{1}{2}AO, Kb = \frac{1}{2}BO, Kc = \frac{1}{2}CO` (§ 47).
  - Sub-circumradii: :math:`R' \cdot AO = R'' \cdot BO = R''' \cdot CO = R^2` (§ 48).
