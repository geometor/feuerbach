Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 19 — Fagnano's Orthic Triangle Perimeter**:
  The feet of the altitudes :math:`M, N, P` form the orthic triangle :math:`MNP` of least perimeter (Fagnano 1775).
  Sides: :math:`NP = a\cos\alpha, MP = b\cos\beta, MN = c\cos\gamma`.
  Perimeter: :math:`MN + MP + NP = \frac{2\Delta}{R}`.
