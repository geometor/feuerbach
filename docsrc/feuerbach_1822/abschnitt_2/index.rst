Second Section: The Orthocenter, Orthic Triangle & Inradius ρ (§§ 19–48)
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — *Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle* (1822, pp. 15–32, Fig. 2–5)

Section II investigates the orthocenter O = X(4) and Fagnano's minimal-perimeter orthic triangle MNP (§ 19). Feuerbach introduces the signed orthic inradius rho = 2R cos alpha cos beta cos gamma as a fundamental third radius invariant (§ 24), discovers that the circumradius of MNP is R/2 (the nine-point radius, § 26), expresses the side-square sum as 8R^2 + 4rho R (§ 27), and proves that the orthocentric sub-triangles BCO, ACO, ABO all share circumradius R (§ 33) and constant power 2rho R (§ 35).
