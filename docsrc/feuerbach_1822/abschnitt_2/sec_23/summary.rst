Section Overview

* **§§ 23–28 — The Orthic Invariant ρ and Nine-Point Radius ½R**:
  - **§ 24**: Orthic inradius :math:`\rho = 2R\cos\alpha\cos\beta\cos\gamma`, with :math:`\triangle MNP : \Delta = \rho : R`.
  - **§ 26**: Circumradius of :math:`\triangle MNP` equals :math:`\frac{1}{2}R` (first appearance of the nine-point radius!).
  - **§§ 27–28**: :math:`a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 8R^2 + 4\rho R`, :math:`\sum r_i^2 = 4R(2R - \rho)`.
