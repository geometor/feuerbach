Section Overview
----------------

* **§§ 30–36 — Exradii and Orthocenter Chord Products**:
  - Perpendiculars :math:`Am, Bn, Cp` are the exradii :math:`\rho^{(1)}, \rho^{(2)}, \rho^{(3)}` of :math:`\triangle MNP`, with :math:`Am + Bn + Cp = \rho + 2R`.
  - Sub-triangle circumradii :math:`R(BCO) = R(ACO) = R(ABO) = R` (§ 33).
  - Power of orthocenter :math:`O`: :math:`AO \cdot OM = BO \cdot ON = CO \cdot OP = 2\rho R` (§ 35).
