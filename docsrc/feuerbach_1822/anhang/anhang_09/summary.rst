Section Overview

* **9th Proposition (9. Satz) — Euclidean Proof of the Nine-Point Circle**:
  *The radius of the circle circumscribed about triangle MNP is half the circumradius of triangle ABC, and its center L bisects the segment OK:*

  .. math::

     LM = LN = LP = LD = LJ = \frac{1}{2}R, \qquad L = \operatorname{mid}(OK).

  Feuerbach explicitly constructs the Euler midpoints :math:`J = \operatorname{mid}(CO)` as antipodes of side midpoints :math:`D = \operatorname{mid}(AB)`!
