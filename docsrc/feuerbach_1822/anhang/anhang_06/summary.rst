6th Proposition (6. Satz): Altitude-Segment Rectangle CO · OP = 2Rρ — Summary
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *6. Satz: Höhenabschnitts-Rechteck CO · OP = 2Rρ*

Section Overview
----------------

* **6th Proposition (6. Satz) — Altitude Chord Rectangle**:
  *The orthocenter O divides each altitude CP into segments whose rectangle equals the circumdiameter times the orthic inradius:*

  .. math::

     CO \cdot OP = 2R\rho.
