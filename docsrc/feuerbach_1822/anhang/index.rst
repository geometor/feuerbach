Synthetic Appendix: Pure Euclidean Proofs of Nine Theorems (pp. 56–62)
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — *Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle* (1822, pp. 56–62, Fig. 13–17)

The Synthetischer Anhang demonstrates the foundational metric properties of the orthocenter, orthic triangle, and nine-point circle purely synthetically from Euclid's Elements Books I–IV and VI without coordinate algebra or trigonometry. Feuerbach proves CO = 2KD (1. Satz), AB^2 + CO^2 = 4R^2 (2. Satz), the orthic perimeter and area ratios (3.–5. Satz), altitude chord rectangle CO · OP = 2Rrho (6. Satz), sum of side squares (7. Satz), Schwarz reflection unfolding of the orthic perimeter (8. Satz), and pure Euclidean proof of the nine-point circle bisecting OK with radius R/2 and explicit Euler midpoint antipodes (9. Satz).
