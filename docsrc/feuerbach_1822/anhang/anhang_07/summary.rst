Section Overview

* **7th Proposition (7. Satz) — Side Squares Sum**:
  *The sum of the squares on the sides of an acute triangle equals twice the square on the circumdiameter plus the rectangle of circumdiameter and orthic diameter:*

  .. math::

     \overline{AB}^2 + \overline{AC}^2 + \overline{BC}^2 = 8R^2 + 4\rho R.
