Section Overview

* **5th Proposition (5. Satz) — Area Ratio**:
  *The area of triangle MNP bears to triangle ABC the same ratio as the inradius of the former bears to the circumradius of the latter:*

  .. math::

     \triangle MNP : \triangle ABC = \rho : R.
