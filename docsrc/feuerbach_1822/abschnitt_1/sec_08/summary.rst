Section Overview

* **§§ 8–11 — Contact Triangles & Center Products**:
  - **§§ 8–9**: Areas of the contact triangles satisfy :math:`\frac{\delta}{r} = \frac{\delta'}{r'} = \frac{\delta''}{r''} = \frac{\delta'''}{r'''} = \frac{\Delta}{2R}` and :math:`\delta' + \delta'' + \delta''' = \delta + 2\Delta`.
  - **§§ 10–11**: Vertex-to-center products satisfy :math:`AS \cdot BS \cdot CS = 4r^2R`, :math:`AS^{(k)} \cdot BS^{(k)} \cdot CS^{(k)} = 4r^{(k)2}R`.
