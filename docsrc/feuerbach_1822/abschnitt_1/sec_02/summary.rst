§ 2: § 2: Expressions of the Four Radii and L'Huilier's Theorem — Summary
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *§. 2. Ausdrücke der vier Halbmesser und Satz von L'Huilier*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 2 — L'Huilier's Area Theorem (1810)**:
  Denoting the radii of the four tritangent circles by :math:`r, r', r'', r'''`, multiplying their values together yields:

  .. math::

     r\,r'\,r''\,r''' = \Delta^2,

  that is: between the rectangle contained by any two of the radii and the rectangle contained by the remaining two, the triangle area :math:`\Delta` is the geometric mean proportional.
