§ 1: § 1: The Four Tritangent Circles and the Excentral Orthocentric System — Summary
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *§. 1. Die vier berührenden Kreise S, S', S'', S'''*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 1 — The Excentral Orthocentric System**:
  For every rectilinear triangle :math:`ABC`, there exist four distinct tritangent circles: one incircle (center :math:`S = X_1`, radius :math:`r`) and three excircles (centers :math:`S', S'', S'''` opposite :math:`A, B, C`, radii :math:`r', r'', r'''`).
  The vertices :math:`A, B, C` are the feet of the altitudes dropped in the excentral triangle :math:`\triangle S'S''S'''` onto its sides, and the incenter :math:`S` is the orthocenter of :math:`\triangle S'S''S'''`.
  Thus, :math:`\triangle ABC` is the orthic (pedal) triangle of the excentral triangle :math:`\triangle S'S''S'''`.
