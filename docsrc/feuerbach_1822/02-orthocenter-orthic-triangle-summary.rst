===============================================================================
Summary — Unit II: Orthocenter, Orthic Triangle & Inradius ρ (§§ 19–48)
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: The Orthic Invariant :math:`\rho = 2R\cos\alpha\cos\beta\cos\gamma` as Third Fundamental Radius

Whereas :person:`Euler <euler>` (1765) expressed triangle-center invariants through :math:`(p, q, r)` and modern texts typically choose :math:`(R, r, s)`, :person:`Feuerbach <feuerbach>` introduces in **§ 24** the signed orthic inradius :math:`\rho = 2R\cos\alpha\cos\beta\cos\gamma`, unlocking clean radius formulas free of :math:`s^2`:

.. list-table:: Core Orthic Identities of Feuerbach's Second Section (pp. 15–32)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 16 44 40

   * - Locus
     - Identity
     - Significance
   * - **§ 19**
     - :math:`MN + MP + NP = \dfrac{2\Delta}{R}`
     - Perimeter of :person:`Fagnano <fagnano>`'s minimal-perimeter :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`
   * - **§§ 23–24**
     - :math:`\rho = 2R\cos\alpha\cos\beta\cos\gamma`, :math:`\dfrac{\triangle MNP}{\Delta} = \dfrac{\rho}{R}`
     - :math:`(A, B, C, O)` are the four tritangent centers of :math:`\triangle MNP`
   * - **§ 26**
     - :math:`R_{MNP} = \dfrac{1}{2}R`
     - Circumradius of :math:`\triangle MNP` equals half the circumradius of :math:`\triangle ABC`
   * - **§§ 27–28**
     - :math:`a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 8R^2 + 4\rho R`, :math:`\sum r_i^2 = 4R(2R - \rho)`
     - Eliminates :math:`s^2` in favor of :math:`(R, \rho)`
   * - **§ 33**
     - :math:`R(BCO) = R(ACO) = R(ABO) = R`
     - Equal circumradii of the three orthocentric sub-triangles
   * - **§ 35**
     - :math:`AO \cdot OM = BO \cdot ON = CO \cdot OP = 2\rho R`
     - Power of :math:`O` with respect to the nine-point and circumcircles
   * - **§ 47**
     - :math:`Ka = \tfrac{1}{2}AO`, :math:`Kb = \tfrac{1}{2}BO`, :math:`Kc = \tfrac{1}{2}CO`
     - Altitude vertex-to-orthocenter segment is twice the circumcenter-to-side distance
