GEOMETOR Commentary & Symbolic Notes

1. The Euler Line and Harmonic Range (O, G; L, K) = -1
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In **§ 60** (p. 40), Feuerbach connects the centroid :math:`G = X(2)` with the orthocenter :math:`O = X(4)`, circumcenter :math:`K = X(3)`, and nine-point center :math:`L = X(5)` along the :term:`Euler line <euler-line>`:

* Segments: :math:`GK = \frac{1}{3}KO`, :math:`GO = \frac{2}{3}KO`, :math:`GL = \frac{1}{6}KO`.
* Harmonic division: The points :math:`(O, G; L, K)` divide the Euler line harmonically with cross-ratio:

  .. math::

     (X_4, X_2; X_5, X_3) = \frac{X_4 - X_5}{X_2 - X_5} : \frac{X_4 - X_3}{X_2 - X_3} = -1.

* Exact incenter-centroid quadrance:

  .. math::

     \overline{GS}^2 = \frac{2}{9}R(2R + \rho) - \frac{2}{3}r(2R - r).
