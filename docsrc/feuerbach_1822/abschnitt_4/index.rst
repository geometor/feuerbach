Fourth Section: The Nine-Point Circle & Feuerbach's Tangency Theorem (§§ 54–60)
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — *Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle* (1822, pp. 37–41, Tab. II Fig. 7)

Section IV forms the historical climax of the entire monograph. Feuerbach establishes that the circumcenter L = X(5) of the orthic triangle MNP bisects the segment KO (§§ 54–55), proves that circle (L, R/2) bisects all three sides of the triangle (§ 56), and proves his immortal Theorem (§ 57): circle (L, R/2) touches the incircle internally at X(11) and the three excircles externally at Fa, Fb, Fc! He closes with the distance sums from L (§§ 58–59) and the harmonic Euler line range (O, G; L, K) = -1 (§ 60).
