§§ 54–57: §§ 54–57: Feuerbach's Nine-Point Circle Tangency Theorem — Summary
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Overview — *§§. 54–57. Der Neunpunktekreis und der Feuerbachsche Berührungssatz*

Section Overview
----------------

* **§§ 54–57 — Feuerbach's Nine-Point Circle Tangency Theorem**:
  - **§ 54**: Center :math:`L = X_5` with radius :math:`\frac{1}{2}R` satisfies :math:`\overline{OL}^2 = \frac{1}{4}R^2 - \rho R = \frac{1}{4}\overline{KO}^2 \implies KO = 2\,OL`.
  - **§ 55**: Points :math:`O, L, K` lie on a single line, with :math:`L` as the midpoint of :math:`KO`.
  - **§ 56**: The circle passes through the three altitude feet and bisects the three sides.
  - **§ 57 (FEUERBACH'S THEOREM)**: Applying Apollonius's median theorem cancels :math:`-2\rho R` identically, yielding:

    .. math::

       LS = \left|\frac{1}{2}R - r\right|, \qquad LS^{(k)} = \frac{1}{2}R + r^{(k)}.

    *The circle passing through the feet of the altitudes touches all four tritangent circles: internally to the incircle at X(11), and externally to the three excircles at Fa, Fb, Fc!*
