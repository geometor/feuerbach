Critical & Symbolic Notes — Unit V: All Nine Points in Feuerbach's 9. Satz (p. 62)
======================================================================================

.. rubric:: Did Feuerbach Already Construct the Euler Midpoints in 1822?

A famous commonplace in secondary histories of mathematics asserts that :person:`Feuerbach <feuerbach>` noticed only *six* points on the :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>` (the three altitude feet :math:`M, N, P` and the three side midpoints :math:`a, b, c`) and overlooked the three Euler midpoints of :math:`AO, BO, CO`.

**Visual inspection of page 62 (`9. Satz`, `fb_082.jpg`) refutes that oversimplification!** Read lines 8–14 of `9. Satz` verbatim:

1. Feuerbach joins the side midpoint :math:`D` of :math:`AB` to the midpoint :math:`L` of :math:`OK`, prolonging :math:`DL` until it meets the altitude :math:`CP` at :math:`J`.
2. By congruent triangles :math:`\triangle OLJ \cong \triangle KLD` :just:`[I. 26] <I.26>`, he proves **both**:

   * :math:`OJ = KD = \frac{1}{2}CO` — **so** :math:`J` **is literally the Euler midpoint** :g:`E_3 <0038.g1:E3>` **bisecting the vertex-to-orthocenter segment** :math:`CO`!
   * :math:`JL = DL = \frac{1}{2}CK = \frac{1}{2}R` — **so** :math:`J` **lies at exact distance** :math:`\frac{1}{2}R` **from** :math:`L`, diametrically opposite the side midpoint :math:`D` on the circle :math:`(L, \frac{1}{2}R)`!

Thus, although Feuerbach states the verbal enunciation of **§ 56** and **9. Satz** in terms of the altitude feet and side midpoints, his synthetic proof on page 62 explicitly constructs the Euler midpoint :math:`J = \operatorname{mid}(CO)` as the antipode of the side midpoint :math:`D = \operatorname{mid}(AB)` with :math:`JL = DL = LP = \frac{1}{2}R`!

**Reflection Unfolding in 8. Satz**: Equally noteworthy on pp. 61–62 is `8. Satz`, where prolonging :math:`PG, PH` to :math:`J, K` on line :math:`MN` unfolds the orthic perimeter :math:`JK = MN + MP + NP` by reflection across :math:`AC` and :math:`BC`—anticipating H. A. Schwarz's celebrated reflection proof of :person:`Fagnano <fagnano>`'s minimal-perimeter problem by more than half a century!
