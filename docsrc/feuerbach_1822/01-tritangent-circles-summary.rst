====================================================================
Summary — Unit I: The Four Tritangent Circle Centers (§§ 1–18)
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Key Invariants of the Tritangent System :math:`(S, S', S'', S''')`

.. list-table:: Fundamental Identities of Feuerbach's First Section (pp. 1–14)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 16 44 40

   * - Locus
     - Identity
     - Geometric Meaning
   * - **§ 1**
     - :math:`S = \operatorname{orthocenter}(\triangle S'S''S''')`
     - :math:`\triangle ABC` is the orthic (pedal) triangle of the excentral triangle :math:`\triangle S'S''S'''`
   * - **§ 2**
     - :math:`rr'r''r''' = \Delta^2`
     - :person:`L'Huilier <lhuilier>`'s symmetric area-radius theorem (1810)
   * - **§§ 3–4**
     - :math:`r'r'' + r'r''' + r''r''' = s^2`, :math:`\sum \frac{1}{r^{(k)}} = \frac{1}{r}`
     - Elementary symmetric polynomials of the three exradii
   * - **§ 5**
     - :math:`r' + r'' + r''' - r = 4R`
     - Sum of signed tritangent radii equals four circumradii
   * - **§ 6**
     - :math:`\sum_{i < j} r_i r_j = ab + ac + bc`
     - Note that :math:`r` enters with negative sign in :math:`(-r) + r' + r'' + r''' = 4R`
   * - **§§ 8–9**
     - :math:`\dfrac{\delta}{r} = \dfrac{\delta^{(k)}}{r^{(k)}} = \dfrac{\Delta}{2R}`, :math:`\delta' + \delta'' + \delta''' = \delta + 2\Delta`
     - Contact triangle areas scale linearly with the four tritangent radii
   * - **§§ 16–17**
     - :math:`SS'^2 + S''S'''^2 = 16R^2`, :math:`\sum_{i<j} S_i S_j^2 = 48R^2`
     - Opposite edges of the orthocentric quadrangle :math:`SS'S''S'''` sum in quadrance to :math:`(4R)^2`
