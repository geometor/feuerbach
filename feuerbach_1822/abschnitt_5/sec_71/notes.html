§§ 71–87: §§ 71–87: Corner Inradii ρ’, ρ’’, ρ’’’ and the Orthocenter of ΔO’O’’O’’’ — Notes¶

Historical, Philological & Symbolic Commentary

GEOMETOR Commentary & Symbolic Notes¶

1. Angle-Doubling, Similarity, and Homothety¶

In any acute triangle \(ABC\) with angles \((\alpha, \beta, \gamma)\):

  • Passing to the contact triangle \(\triangle DEF\) replaces angles by \((90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\alpha, 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\beta, 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\gamma)\).

  • Passing from \(\triangle DEF\) to its orthic triangle \(\triangle mnp\) applies \(\theta \mapsto 180^\circ - 2\theta\), restoring the original angles:

    \[180^\circ - 2\left(90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\alpha\right) = \alpha!\]

  • Furthermore, by the tangent-chord theorem [III. 32], every side of \(\triangle mnp\) is parallel to the corresponding side of \(\triangle ABC\), yielding a pure homothety with ratio \(\frac{r}{2R}\) and area ratio \(\frac{r^2}{4R^2}\).

2. Exact Verification of § 64 on the Canonical (15, 13, 14) Triangle¶

With \(\Delta = 84\), \(R = \frac{65}{8}\), \(4R^2 = \frac{4225}{16}\), \((r, r_a, r_b, r_c) = (4, 14, \frac{21}{2}, 12)\), and \(\rho = \frac{99}{52}\):

  • Sum of derived contact-orthic areas: \(\sum \mu^{(k)} = \frac{125328}{845}\).

  • Orthic area: \(\triangle MNP = \frac{\rho}{R}\Delta = \frac{16632}{845}\).

  • Total: \(\sum \mu^{(k)} + \triangle MNP = \frac{141960}{845} = 168 = 2\Delta\).
