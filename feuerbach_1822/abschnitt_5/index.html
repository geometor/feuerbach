Fifth Section: Comparative Proportions Among Derived Triangles (§§ 61–87)¶

Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle (1822, pp. 41–55, Fig. 8–12)

Section V establishes harmonic and homothetic proportions among the contact triangles and orthic triangles. Feuerbach proves that the orthic triangle MNP is the mean proportional between triangle ABC and the contact triangle def (§ 61), derives the fourfold geometric-mean chain mu : delta = delta : Delta (§ 63), proves the five-triangle area partition law mu + mu’ + mu’’ + mu’’’ + area(MNP) = 2Delta (§ 64), and concludes in § 87 that incenter S is the orthocenter of triangle O’O’’O’’’.
