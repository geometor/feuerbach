§§ 41–48: §§ 41–48: Corner Orthocenters M’, N’, P’ and Circumcenter Sub-Circles — Summary¶

Mathematical Overview — §§. 41–48. Höhenschnitte M’, N’, P’ der Eckdreiecke und Teilumkreise R’, R’’, R’’’

Parallel Reading Tracks¶

  • English: §§ 41–48 (p. 28)

  • Deutsch: §§ 41–48 (S. 28)

Section Overview¶

  • §§ 41–48 — Corner Triangles and Circumcenter Sub-Circles: - Orthocenters \(M', N', P'\) of corner triangles satisfy \(AM' + BN' + CP' = 2(R - \rho)\). - Circumcenter distances \(Ka = \frac{1}{2}AO, Kb = \frac{1}{2}BO, Kc = \frac{1}{2}CO\) (§ 47). - Sub-circumradii: \(R' \cdot AO = R'' \cdot BO = R''' \cdot CO = R^2\) (§ 48).
