Critical & Symbolic Notes — Unit III: Exact SymPy Audit of §§ 49–60 & The § 51 Sign Slip¶

Why Symbolic Verification Matters in Historical Editions

1. Discovery & Diagnosis of Feuerbach’s § 51 / § 52 / § 60 Sign-Substitution Slip¶

When encoding every equation of Feuerbach (1822) §§ 49–60 in geometor.feuerbach.euler.prove_euler_1765_identities() over \(\mathbb{Q}(a, b, c)\) in sympy, a striking philological fact emerged on pages 35, 36, and 40–41:

  • In § 51 (p. 35), Feuerbach proves directly from coordinates the 100% exact incenter quadrance

    \[\overline{OS}^2 = 2r^2 - 2\rho R.\]

  • Immediately below on p. 35, he applies his customary substitution \((a, b, c) \mapsto (-a, b, c)\) (introduced in § 8, p. 6, where he noted r wird zu r' und +R zu -R) to write

    \[\overline{OS^{(k)}}^2 \stackrel{\text{1822 text}}{=} 2r^{(k)2} + 2\rho R \qquad \text{(printed slip!)}.\]

  • Why this fails algebraically: By § 24 (p. 18) and \(R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta}\),

    \[\rho = \frac{(-a^2+b^2+c^2)(a^2-b^2+c^2)(a^2+b^2-c^2)}{16abc\Delta}, \qquad \rho R = \frac{(-a^2+b^2+c^2)(a^2-b^2+c^2)(a^2+b^2-c^2)}{64\Delta^2}.\]

    Under \(a \mapsto -a\) (holding \(+\Delta\) positive), both \(R\) and \(\rho\) reverse sign (\(R \mapsto -R\) and \(\rho \mapsto -\rho\)), so their product \(\rho R\)—which depends solely on \(a^2, b^2, c^2\)—is strictly invariant!

2. Why § 57 (Feuerbach’s Theorem) and § 59 Are 100% Unscathed!¶

Why did the § 51 sign slip fail to corrupt § 57 or § 59? Look at the exact order of operations on page 38 (fb_058.jpg):

  1. First, for the incenter \(S\) (where \(\overline{OS}^2 = 2r^2 - 2\rho R\) is 100% exact!), Apollonius’s median theorem \(2\overline{LS}^2 + 2\overline{OL}^2 = \overline{KS}^2 + \overline{OS}^2\) cancels \(-2\rho R\) against \(2\overline{OL}^2 = \frac{1}{2}R^2 - 2\rho R\), leaving \(LS = \frac{1}{2}R - r\) with \(\rho\) eliminated.

  2. Second, rather than substituting his § 51 expression for \(\overline{OS'}^2\) into the median theorem (which would have left a spurious \(+4\rho R\)), Feuerbach substituted \(a, b, c \mapsto -a, -b, -c\) directly into \(LS = \frac{1}{2}R - r\), where \(\rho\) was already gone! Thus \(R \mapsto -R\) and \(r \mapsto r^{(k)}\) yielded the completely exact \(LS^{(k)} = \frac{1}{2}R + r^{(k)}\), and squaring those exact expressions in § 59 gave the exact sum \(13R^2 - 4\rho R\) (which also validates \(\sum \overline{OS_i}^2 = 16R(R - \rho)\) via \(2\sum \overline{LS_i}^2 + 8\overline{OL}^2 = \sum \overline{KS_i}^2 + \sum \overline{OS_i}^2\)!).

3. Compositorial Typo in § 55 (p. 37) and Nine-Point History¶

  • p. 37, § 55, line 6: Prints OL^2 = 1/2 R^2 - rho R where § 54 (three lines above on p. 37) established OL^2 = 1/4 R^2 - rho R; the immediately following deduction LP^2 - OL^2 = OP . Kc = rho R uses the true 1/4 R^2 - rho R.

  • From Six Points to Nine Points (1821–1842): In §§ 54–56 (and synthetically in 9. Satz), Feuerbach explicitly proves concyclicity of the six points (three altitude feet + three side midpoints), while Brianchon and Poncelet (1821) independently proved concyclicity of all nine points (adding the three Euler midpoints E1, E2, E3 of \(AO, BO, CO\)), and Olry Terquem (1842) coined the name cercle des neuf points (nine-point circle). Notably, Feuerbach already knew \(AO = 2\,Ka\) (§ 47) and \(L = \operatorname{mid}(KO)\) (§ 55), from which \(LE_a = LK_a\)? Rather, \(LE_a = \frac{1}{2}AK = \frac{1}{2}R\) follows in a single line by midline homothety!
