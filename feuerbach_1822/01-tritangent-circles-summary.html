Summary — Unit I: The Four Tritangent Circle Centers (§§ 1–18)¶

Key Invariants of the Tritangent System \((S, S', S'', S''')\)

Fundamental Identities of Feuerbach’s First Section (pp. 1–14)¶

Locus

Identity

Geometric Meaning

§ 1

\(S = \operatorname{orthocenter}(\triangle S'S''S''')\)

\(\triangle ABC\) is the orthic (pedal) triangle of the excentral triangle \(\triangle S'S''S'''\)

§ 2

\(rr'r''r''' = \Delta^2\)

L'Huilier’s symmetric area-radius theorem (1810)

§§ 3–4

\(r'r'' + r'r''' + r''r''' = s^2\), \(\sum \frac{1}{r^{(k)}} = \frac{1}{r}\)

Elementary symmetric polynomials of the three exradii

§ 5

\(r' + r'' + r''' - r = 4R\)

Sum of signed tritangent radii equals four circumradii

§ 6

\(\sum_{i < j} r_i r_j = ab + ac + bc\)

Note that \(r\) enters with negative sign in \((-r) + r' + r'' + r''' = 4R\)

§§ 8–9

\(\dfrac{\delta}{r} = \dfrac{\delta^{(k)}}{r^{(k)}} = \dfrac{\Delta}{2R}\), \(\delta' + \delta'' + \delta''' = \delta + 2\Delta\)

Contact triangle areas scale linearly with the four tritangent radii

§§ 16–17

\(SS'^2 + S''S'''^2 = 16R^2\), \(\sum_{i<j} S_i S_j^2 = 48R^2\)

Opposite edges of the orthocentric quadrangle \(SS'S''S'''\) sum in quadrance to \((4R)^2\)
