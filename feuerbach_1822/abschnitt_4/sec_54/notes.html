§§ 54–57: §§ 54–57: Feuerbach’s Nine-Point Circle Tangency Theorem — Notes¶

Historical, Philological & Symbolic Commentary

GEOMETOR Commentary & Symbolic Notes¶

1. Why § 57 (Feuerbach’s Tangency Theorem) and § 59 Are 100% Unscathed!¶

Crucially, Feuerbach’s algebraic slip on the excenters in § 51 has zero impact on the validity of the Nine-Point Tangency Theorem in § 57:

  • In § 57, Feuerbach applies Apollonius’s median theorem in \(\triangle KOS\):

    \[2\overline{LS}^2 + 2\overline{OL}^2 = \overline{KS}^2 + \overline{OS}^2.\]

    Substituting \(\overline{OL}^2 = \frac{1}{4}R^2 - \rho R\), \(\overline{KS}^2 = R^2 - 2rR\), and the exact incenter distance \(\overline{OS}^2 = 2r^2 - 2\rho R\):

    \[2\overline{LS}^2 + 2\left(\frac{1}{4}R^2 - \rho R\right) = (R^2 - 2rR) + (2r^2 - 2\rho R).\]

    Observe that the term \(-2\rho R\) cancels identically from both sides of the equation!

  • Thus \(\overline{LS}^2 = (\frac{1}{2}R - r)^2\), yielding \(LS = |\frac{1}{2}R - r|\).

  • Applying the sign substitution \(a \mapsto -a\) to this simplified equation (which no longer contains \(\rho\)!) sends \(R \mapsto -R\) and \(r \mapsto r^{(k)}\), yielding the exact excenter distances:

    \[LS^{(k)} = \frac{1}{2}R + r^{(k)}.\]

    Feuerbach’s Theorem is 100% algebraically and geometrically exact!

2. Exact Coordinates & Benchmarks on the Canonical (15, 13, 14) Triangle¶

In geometor.feuerbach.tangency:

  • Nine-point center \(X(5) = (6, \frac{63}{16})\) with radius \(R_9 = \frac{65}{16}\).

  • Incenter \(X(1) = (6, 4)\) with radius \(r = 4\).

  • Inner tangency point: Feuerbach point \(X(11) = (6, 8)\).

  • Distance \(X(5)X(1) = |\frac{65}{16} - 4| = \frac{1}{16}\), so quadrance \(Q(X_5, X_1) = \frac{1}{256}\).

  • Excircle tangency points: \(Fe_a = (\frac{2709}{289}, \frac{1792}{289})\), \(Fe_b = (\frac{553}{233}, \frac{1344}{233})\), \(Fe_c = (\frac{1672}{257}, \frac{-24}{257})\).
