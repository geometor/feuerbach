7th Proposition (7. Satz): Sum of Squared Sides AB² + AC² + BC² = 8R² + 4ρR — Notes¶

Historical, Philological & Symbolic Commentary

GEOMETOR Commentary & Symbolic Notes¶

1. Did Feuerbach Already Construct the Euler Midpoints in 1822?¶

A widespread claim in mathematical history asserts that Feuerbach noticed only six points on the nine-point circle (the three altitude feet and three side midpoints) and that the three Euler midpoints were only added later by Olry Terquem (1842).

Inspection of page 62 (`9. Satz`) refutes that claim!

Feuerbach’s synthetic construction explicitly draws line \(DL\) from side midpoint \(D = \operatorname{mid}(AB)\) through \(L = \operatorname{mid}(OK)\) to meet altitude \(CP\) at \(J\). By congruent triangles \(\triangle OLJ \cong \triangle KLD\) [I. 26]:

  • \(OJ = KD = \frac{1}{2}CO\) — so \(J\) is literally the Euler midpoint E3 bisecting \(CO\)!

  • \(JL = DL = \frac{1}{2}CK = \frac{1}{2}R\) — so \(J\) lies at distance \(\frac{1}{2}R\) from \(L\), diametrically opposite \(D\)!

2. Reflection Unfolding in 8. Satz (Schwarz Anticipation)¶

In 8. Satz (pp. 61–62), dropping perpendiculars \(PG, PH\) from an altitude foot \(P\) onto the other two sides and prolonging them to \(J, K\) unfolds the perimeter of \(\triangle MNP\) into a single straight line \(JK = 2\,GH\). This reflection construction directly anticipated H. A. Schwarz’s famous 1890 reflection solution of Fagnano’s problem!
