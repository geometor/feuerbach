Third Section: Distances from the Circumcenter and Orthocenter (§§ 49–53)¶

Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle (1822, pp. 33–36)

Section III determines the exact mutual quadrances between the circumcenter K = X(3), orthocenter O = X(4), and the four tritangent centers S, S’, S’’, S’’’. Feuerbach cites Euler (1765) and Fuß (1797) for KS^2 = R^2 - 2rR (§ 49), proves sum(KS_i^2) = 12R^2 (§ 50), deduces OS^2 = 2r^2 - 2rho R (§ 51), and proves the foundational distance KO^2 = R^2 - 4rho R (§ 53).
