Giovanni Francesco Fagnano dei Toschi
=====================================

*Italian Mathematician*

Proved in 1775 that among all triangles inscribed in an acute triangle :math:`\triangle ABC`, the orthic triangle :math:`\triangle MNP` minimizes perimeter—explicitly cited by Feuerbach in § 19 (p. 15).
