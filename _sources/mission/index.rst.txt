Mission & Architectural Principles
==================================

.. rubric:: Uniting Archival Folio Fidelity with 100% Exact Symbolic Algebra

``geometor-feuerbach`` bridges the Digital Scriptorium and constructive symbolic engine pillars of GEOMETOR:

1. **Multimodal Folio Verification Over OCR:** Every Latin formula in :person:`Leonhard Euler <euler>`'s 1767 *Novi Commentarii* printing (**E325**, pp. 103–123, ``Tab. II``) and every German proposition in :person:`Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach <feuerbach>`'s 1822 Nuremberg monograph (pp. I–XVI, 1–62, ``Tab. I–II``) was transcribed and checked directly against high-resolution archival scans—recovering both Euler's 1767 St. Petersburg typesetting *errata* (§ 7 ``bc`` pro ``6c``; § 12 ``=`` pro ``-``) and Feuerbach's 1822 sign-substitution slip in § 51 (:math:`+2\rho R` pro :math:`-2\rho R` in :math:`\overline{OS^{(k)}}^2`).
2. **Zero-Float Symbolic Verification:** Every center, circle, and tangency point is constructed over exact :mod:`sympy` rationals on ``A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), C = (5, 12)`` using ``Model(..., auto_intersect=False)`` and closed-form radical-axis intersections.
3. **Ecosystem Integration:** Cross-references cevian concurrency (:cite:`Ceva 1678 <ceva_1678>`), projective collinearity (:cite:`Menelaus <menelaus_sphaerica>`), Euclidean foundations (:cite:`Euclid <euclid_elements>`), modern college geometry (:cite:`Altshiller-Court <altshiller_court_1952>`), and :cite:`Kimberling's ETC <kimberling_etc>`.
