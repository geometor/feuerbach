Section Overview

* **§§ 71–87 — Corner Inradii and Orthocenter S**:
  - Corner inradii: :math:`\rho' = r\cos\alpha, \rho'' = r\cos\beta, \rho''' = r\cos\gamma`.
  - Triangle :math:`O'O''O'''` is congruent to :math:`\triangle DEF` with parallel sides.
  - Closing theorem (**§ 87**): The incenter :math:`S` of :math:`\triangle ABC` is simultaneously the orthocenter of triangle :math:`O'O''O'''`!
