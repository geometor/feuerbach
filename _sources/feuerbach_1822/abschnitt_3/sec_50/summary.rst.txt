Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 50 — Sum of Circumcenter Quadrances**:
  Summing the four squared distances from :math:`K` via :math:`r' + r'' + r''' - r = 4R`:

  .. math::

     \overline{KS}^2 + \overline{KS'}^2 + \overline{KS''}^2 + \overline{KS'''}^2 = 12R^2 = 3(2R)^2.
