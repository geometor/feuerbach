Section Overview
----------------

* **§ 49 — Euler & Fuß Incenter–Circumcenter Formulas**:
  - Distance from circumcenter :math:`K` to incenter :math:`S`: :math:`\overline{KS}^2 = R^2 - 2rR` (Euler 1765 § 18 VI, Fuß 1797 § 32).
  - Distances to excenters: :math:`\overline{KS^{(k)}}^2 = R^2 + 2r^{(k)}R`.
