Section Overview
----------------

* **§§ 58–60 — Center Distance Sums and Centroid G**:
  - **§ 58**: Linear sum: :math:`LS + LS' + LS'' + LS''' = 6R = 3(2R)`.
  - **§ 59**: Quadratic sum: :math:`\sum \overline{LS_i}^2 = R(13R - 4\rho)`.
  - **§ 60**: The centroid :math:`G = X_2` lies on :math:`KO` with :math:`GK = \frac{1}{3}KO`, forming the harmonic range :math:`(O, G; L, K) = -1`.
