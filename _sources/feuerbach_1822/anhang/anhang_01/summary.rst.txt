Section Overview
----------------

* **Propositions 1–4 — Euclidean Metric Lemmas**:
  - **1. Satz**: :math:`CO = 2\,KD` (altitude segment is twice circumcenter distance to opposite side).
  - **2. Satz**: :math:`\overline{AB}^2 + \overline{CO}^2 = 4R^2 = (2R)^2`.
  - **3.–4. Satz**: :math:`r(AB + AC + BC) = R(MN + MP + NP) \iff MN + MP + NP = \frac{2\Delta}{R}`.
