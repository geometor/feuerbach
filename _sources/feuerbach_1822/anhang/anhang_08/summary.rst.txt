Section Overview

* **8th Proposition (8. Satz) — Orthic Perimeter Unfolding**:
  *Dropping perpendiculars PG, PH from any altitude foot P onto the other two sides yields pedal segment GH equal to the semiperimeter of triangle MNP:*

  .. math::

     GH = \frac{1}{2}(MN + MP + NP).
