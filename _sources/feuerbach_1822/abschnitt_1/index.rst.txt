First Section: On the Centers of the Four Tritangent Circles (§§ 1–18)
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach — *Properties of Several Remarkable Points of the Rectilinear Triangle* (1822, pp. 1–14, Fig. 1)

Section I establishes the metric foundations of the four tritangent circles (one incircle, three excircles). Feuerbach discovers that triangle ABC is the orthic (pedal) triangle of the excentral triangle S'S''S''', with incenter S as its orthocenter (§ 1). He proves L'Huilier's theorem rr'r''r''' = Delta^2 (§ 2), establishes elementary symmetric polynomial identities among the exradii (§§ 3–7), proves that contact triangle areas scale linearly with the tritangent radii (§§ 8–11), and deduces the metric laws governing the orthocentric quadrangle (S, S', S'', S''') with opposite distance-square sums equal to (4R)^2 = 16R^2 (§§ 12–18).
