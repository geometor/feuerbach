=====================================================================================
Summary — Unit III: The Nine-Point Circle & Feuerbach's Tangency Theorem (§§ 49–60)
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Logical Chain of Feuerbach's Masterpiece (pp. 33–41)

1. **Euler–Fuß Quadrances from Circumcenter K (§§ 49–50)**:
   :math:`\overline{KS}^2 = R^2 - 2rR` (:person:`Euler <euler>` E325 § 18 VI; :person:`Fuß <fuss>` 1797 § 32) and :math:`\overline{KS^{(k)}}^2 = R^2 + 2r^{(k)}R`, summing via :math:`r' + r'' + r''' - r = 4R` to :math:`\sum \overline{KS_i}^2 = 12R^2`.
2. **Orthocenter-Incenter & Orthocenter-Circumcenter Quadrances (§§ 51, 53)**:
   Direct coordinate-projection calculation on base :math:`AB = c` yields :math:`\overline{OS}^2 = 2r^2 - 2\rho R` and :math:`\overline{KO}^2 = R^2 - 4\rho R`.
3. **Nine-Point Center L = X(5) Bisecting KO (§§ 54–56)**:
   Because :math:`O` is the incenter of :math:`\triangle MNP` (inradius :math:`\rho`) and :math:`L` is its circumcenter (circumradius :math:`\frac{1}{2}R`), applying Euler's :math:`R^2 - 2rR` formula inside :math:`\triangle MNP` gives :math:`\overline{OL}^2 = \frac{1}{4}R^2 - \rho R = \frac{1}{4}\overline{KO}^2`. Projection onto :math:`AB` proves :math:`LJ = \frac{1}{2}(OP + Kc)`, establishing that :math:`L` is the exact **midpoint** of :math:`KO` and that circle :math:`(L, \frac{1}{2}R)` bisects all three sides :math:`BC, AC, AB`.
4. **Apollonius's Median Law & Feuerbach's Theorem (§ 57)**:
   Applying :math:`2\overline{LS}^2 + 2\overline{OL}^2 = \overline{KS}^2 + \overline{OS}^2` in :math:`\triangle KOS` cancels :math:`-2\rho R` identically, leaving

   .. math::

      LS = \left|\frac{1}{2}R - r\right|, \qquad LS^{(k)} = \frac{1}{2}R + r^{(k)},

   proving internal tangency to the incircle at :math:`X(11)` and external tangency to all three excircles!
5. **Euler Line Harmonic Range (§ 60)**:
   On the :term:`Euler line <euler-line>`, :math:`(O, G, L, K) = (X_4, X_2, X_5, X_3)` satisfy :math:`OL : LG : GK = 3 : 1 : 2` and cross-ratio :math:`(O, G; L, K) = -1`.
