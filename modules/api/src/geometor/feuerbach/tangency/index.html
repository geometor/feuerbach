src.geometor.feuerbach.tangency¶

Exact Euclidean construction and deductive proof of Feuerbach’s Theorem (1822, § 57).

Constructs on the canonical Heronian (15, 13, 14) triangle A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), C = (5, 12):

  • Side midpoints Ma, Mb, Mc (medial triangle),

  • Altitude feet Ha, Hb, Hc (orthic triangle MNP) and orthocenter X4 = (5, 15/4),

  • Euler midpoints Ea, Eb, Ec bisecting AX4, BX4, CX4,

  • Circumcenter X3 = (7, 33/8), centroid X2 = (19/3, 4), and nine-point center X5 = (6, 63/16) along the harmonic Euler range (X4, X2; X5, X3) = -1,

  • The nine-point circle passing through all nine canonical points (radius 65/16),

  • Incenter X1 = (6, 4) with its incircle (radius 4) and excenters Ja = (21, 14), Jb = (-7, 21/2), Jc = (8, -12) with their three excircles,

  • The internal tangency Feuerbach point X11 = (6, 8) and the three external excircle tangency points Fea = (2709/289, 1792/289), Feb = (553/233, 1344/233), and Fec = (1672/257, -24/257) computed directly via intersect().
