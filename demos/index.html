Exact Symbolic Demonstrations¶

100% Exact SymPy Polynomial Proofs & geometor.model Compass-and-Straightedge Constructions

Unlike floating-point dynamic geometry sketches, every theorem in Euler (1765) and Feuerbach (1822) is executed and verified symbolically in geometor.feuerbach with zero floating-point approximations.

Canonical Heronian (15, 13, 14) Rational Locus¶

Placing the vertices of the acute scalene Heronian triangle with sides \(a = 15, b = 13, c = 14\) (area \(\Delta = 84\), semiperimeter \(s = 21\)) at integer Cartesian coordinates A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), C = (5, 12) places all classical centers, tritangent centers, and Feuerbach tangencies strictly inside \(\mathbb{Q}^2\):

  • Incenter & Incircle: S = X1 = (6, 4), inradius \(r = 4\).

  • Centroid: F = G = X(2) = (19/3, 4).

  • Circumcenter & Circumcircle: H = K = X(3) = (7, 33/8), circumradius \(R = \frac{65}{8}\).

  • Orthocenter & Orthic Inradius: E = O = X(4) = (5, 15/4), orthic inradius \(\rho = \frac{99}{52}\).

  • Nine-Point Center & Circle: L = X5 = (6, 63/16), radius \(R_9 = \frac{65}{16}\).

  • Internal Tangency (Feuerbach Point): X11 = (6, 8), where \(Q(X_5, X_1) = \left(\frac{65}{16} - 4\right)^2 = \frac{1}{256}\).

  • Three External Excircle Tangencies: Fea = (2709/289, 1792/289), Feb = (553/233, 1344/233), and Fec = (1672/257, -24/257), forming the Feuerbach Triangle \triangle Fea Feb Fec.

Demonstration II: demos/feuerbach_tangency.py¶

Constructs the nine-point circle, all four tritangent circles, X(11), and Fea, Feb, Fec via Model(..., auto_intersect=False) and model.intersect(...), verifying the harmonic Euler cross-ratio (X4, X2; X5, X3) = -1 and exporting the four interactive .euclid-graphic SVGs:

 1"""Executable demonstration of Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach's Theorem (1822, § 57).
         2
         3Constructs the nine-point circle, the four tritangent circles, the Feuerbach point X(11),
         4and the three excircle tangency points Fea, Feb, Fec in exact rational coordinates on
         5A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), C = (5, 12) using geometor.model.Model(..., auto_intersect=False),
         6and exports interactive .euclid-graphic SVGs.

        18from geometor.feuerbach.tangency import construct_feuerbach_tangency

        32    r_in = res.incircle.radius

        55        )

        57        "Harmonic Euler Range",
        58        f"(X₄, X₂; X₅, X₃) = {res.harmonic_cross_ratio}",
